# R-John: Campaign Arc - 7-Day Structure

## Three-Act Structure

### Act 1: Setup (Días 1-2)
**Día 1: The Office** - Establecer poder y relaciones (Maria, Laura, Emma)
**Día 2: The Charity Gala** - Social politics, first impressions (Jessica, Olivia, Victoria)

### Act 2: Rising Action (Días 3-5)
**Día 3: The Campaign Trail** - Pressure, debate prep (Isabella)
**Día 4: Capitol Hill** - Legislation, principles (Natalie, Olivia)
**Día 5: The Fundraiser** - Temptation, moral compromise (Ashley, Victoria)

### Act 3: Climax & Resolution (Días 6-7)
**Día 6: The Scandal** - Crisis, damage control (Maria, Laura, Emma)
**Día 7: Resolution** - Consequences, judgment, legacy (Todos los NPCs)

## Stakes Progression
- **Día 1-2:** Personal stakes (first impressions, relationships)
- **Día 3-5:** Political stakes (campaign, policy, funding)
- **Día 6-7:** Existential stakes (career, legacy, everything)

## 12 Endings
- **Victory (2):** Statesman, Power Broker
- **Damaged (2):** Cautious Politician, Private Life
- **Strategic (2):** Reinvention, Scandalized Exit
- **Downfall (2):** Disgraced, Destroyed
- **Secret (4):** Kingmaker, Redemption, Untouchable, Truth & Reconciliation

**Todos los finales son válidos, no hay ending "canon".**
