# R-John: Character Arcs (10 NPCs)

## 1. Senator John (Protagonist)
- **Inicio:** Idealistic, wants to make difference
- **Conflicto:** Power vs. Integrity (player-determined)
- **Arco:** Depends on choices (Victory → maintains integrity, Downfall → succumbs)
- **Resolución:** Legacy defined by ending

## 2. Maria Santos (Press Secretary, 35)
- **Inicio:** Professional, ambitious, loyal
- **Conflicto:** Career advancement vs. personal feelings
- **Arco:** Professional respect → potential romance/loyalty
- **Resolución:** High trust = protective, Low trust = distant

## 3. Laura Mitchell (Personal Assistant, 28)
- **Inicio:** Naive, earnest, devoted
- **Conflicto:** Innocence vs. harsh reality
- **Arco:** Innocence → experience (or protected)
- **Resolución:** Protected = continues working, Exposed = worldly or disillusioned

## 4. Emma Thompson (Intern, 24)
- **Inicio:** Idealistic, nervous, eager
- **Conflicto:** Idealism vs. pragmatism
- **Arco:** Idealism → pragmatism (or disillusionment)
- **Resolución:** Mentored = empowered, Exploited = disillusioned

## 5. Jessica Chen (TV Journalist, 32)
- **Inicio:** Sharp, ambitious, ethical
- **Conflicto:** Professional curiosity vs. personal investment
- **Arco:** Professional → personal (if engaged)
- **Resolución:** Professional = fair coverage, Personal = sympathetic

## 6. Olivia Parker (Party Colleague, 40)
- **Inicio:** Professional, supportive, pragmatic
- **Conflicto:** Party loyalty vs. personal principles
- **Arco:** Colleague → ally (or rival if betrayed)
- **Resolución:** Ally = trusted advisor, Betrayed = alliance broken

## 7. Victoria Ashford (Wealthy Donor, 62)
- **Inicio:** Calculating, powerful, demanding
- **Conflicto:** Transactional vs. genuine respect
- **Arco:** Transactional → manipulative (or respectful)
- **Resolución:** Compromised = expects votes, Principled = respects or opposes

## 8. Isabella Rodriguez (Campaign Manager, 38)
- **Inicio:** Strategic, driven, focused
- **Conflicto:** Professional success vs. personal investment
- **Arco:** Professional → personally invested
- **Resolución:** Professional = successful campaign, Personal = relationship deepens

## 9. Natalie Foster (Fellow Senator, 45)
- **Inicio:** Principled, intellectual, respected
- **Conflicto:** Principles vs. political reality
- **Arco:** Respect → partnership (or opposition)
- **Resolución:** Aligned = collaboration, Compromised = disappointed

## 10. Ashley Monroe (Lobbyist, 35)
- **Inicio:** Bold, flirtatious, unapologetic
- **Conflicto:** Temptation vs. consequences
- **Arco:** Temptation → consequence (or ally if resisted)
- **Resolución:** Succumbed = liability, Resisted = respects John
