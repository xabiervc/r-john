# R-John: Power & Scandal
## Game Design Document - Premium Edition

**Target:** The Game Awards, D.I.C.E., BAFTA Games  
**Genre:** Political Drama Visual Novel  
**Playtime:** 3-5 hours  

## Core Experience
Seven-day political drama as Senator John navigating power, relationships, and scandal.

## 12 Endings
Victory (2), Damaged (2), Strategic (2), Downfall (2), Secret (4)

## Accessibility
Text scaling, high contrast, content warnings, 3 difficulty modes, keyboard nav

## Systems
Stats: Reputation, Lust, Risk (0-100)  
Relationships: Trust, Respect, Attraction, Loyalty per NPC  
Consequences: Immediate, delayed, cascading
