# Art, UI and Audio Brief

## Visual pillars
- 2.5D characters over hand-painted 2D backgrounds.
- Public spaces use cool blue-grey-white palettes; private reflection scenes use amber and deep shadow without sexualized framing.
- Satire is conveyed through composition, signage, media screens and character posture, not caricatures of protected traits.

## Characters
Create turnarounds for R. John and each named NPC. For each: neutral, relaxed, stressed, angry, and resolute expressions; seated/standing poses; public-event outfit; travel outfit; and accessibility-safe silhouettes. Adult characters must read as adults.

## Environments
Required sets: office, TV studio, press room, charity gala, travel lounge, hotel lobby, congress hall, private meeting room, parliament chamber, and apartment. Each receives day/evening lighting variants, navigation hotspots, crowd density tiers, evidence-placement slots, and accessibility contrast review.

## UI
HUD: Reputation, Pressure level, current time slot, location, Public/Private Mask indicator, and phone. Dialogue uses separate treatments for spoken line, internal thought, consent/boundary confirmation, and state change. Never hide a consequential consent or evidence warning behind color alone.

## Audio
Public mode: restrained orchestral/piano motifs. Investigation: sparse percussion, newsroom ambience and controlled tension. Mask change: short filtered transition. Provide captions for all critical effects and separate sliders for music, SFX, dialogue and UI.

## Deliverable checklist
- Character sheets and expression atlas for each character
- Location paintovers with lighting variants
- UI flow wireframes for HUD, phone, dialogue, evidence ledger and ending card
- Animation list: idle, gesture, phone, look-away, stress/fidget, resolve
- Audio cue sheet with event IDs and caption text
- Asset naming convention: `{category}_{entity}_{state}_{variant}`
