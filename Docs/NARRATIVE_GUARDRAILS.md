# Narrative Guardrails

## Canonical content rules
- Every intimate or romantic interaction involves adults, voluntary participation, clear affirmative consent and an immediate option to stop.
- A political, employment, media, financial or hospitality power imbalance cannot be used as a route to obtain consent.
- Rejection never carries retaliation, reputational punishment or gameplay penalties for the NPC.
- Bribery, intimidation, blackmail, threats, evidence destruction and obstruction are not success strategies. They trigger accountability, investigation or loss routes.
- Investigation gameplay focuses on facts, documents, public statements and lawful process.

## Dialogue requirements
Every relationship dialogue must contain: an unambiguous stop/decline branch, a neutral exit, state effects, and an explicit consent checkpoint before a private scene.

## QA gates
No dialogue tree may grant rewards after a refusal, permit escalation after a withdrawal, or treat professional leverage as attraction.
