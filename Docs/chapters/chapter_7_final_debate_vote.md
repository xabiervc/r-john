# Chapter 7 — Final Debate & Vote

## Purpose
Sandbox/climax chapter. All routes converge around a televised debate, parliamentary vote and one of eight ending families.

## Mandatory beats
- Afternoon: `final_debate` at `tv_studio`. Debate scoring uses factual consistency, policy preparation and pressure band.
- Evening: `final_vote` at `parliament`. Vote outcome depends on coalition trust, debate score and scandal state.

## Optional routes
- Emma, Jessica and Olivia can appear only in voluntary, adult, clearly consented relationship or professional-support scenes. A rejection or withdrawal of consent always ends the route immediately and safely.
- Sophie may publish an accountability update, a corrected record, or the final exposé based on evidence level.
- Carlos may be defeated politically through verified evidence and debate performance, never through blackmail.

## Ending families
1. Accountable victory: vote passes, reputation recovers through verified reform.
2. Narrow survival: vote outcome mixed, career remains viable.
3. Exposed scandal: evidence corroborates misconduct and career collapses.
4. Public accountability: resignation and independent inquiry.
5. Personal reset: voluntary withdrawal from politics and relationships handled respectfully.
6. Ongoing scrutiny: career continues under oversight.
7. Forced resignation: coalition support collapses.
8. Reform mandate: exceptional debate, transparency and vote victory.

## Final calculation inputs
`reputation`, `pressure`, `debate_score`, `vote_support`, `sophie_evidence_level`, `documented_contradictions`, `consent_violations`, `accountability_actions`, and `relationship_status`.
