## 📊 Game Systems

### Primary Stats (0-100)
- **Reputation** - Public image and political capital
- **Lust** - Relationship/sexual tension
- **Risk** - Scandal exposure probability

### Relationship Tracking
Per-NPC stats: Trust, Respect, Attraction, Loyalty

### Consequence System
- **Immediate** - Stat changes now
- **Delayed** - Effects in future chapters
- **Cascading** - Multiple related consequences
- **Hidden** - Surprise twists

---

## 📖 Story Structure

### Seven-Day Arc
1. **The Office** - Meet your staff
2. **The Charity Gala** - Social politics
3. **The Campaign Trail** - Debate prep
4. **Capitol Hill** - Legislation
5. **The Fundraiser** - Temptation
6. **The Scandal** - Crisis
7. **Resolution** - Consequences

### 12 Endings
- **Victory:** Statesman, Power Broker
- **Damaged:** Cautious Politician, Private Life
- **Strategic:** Reinvention, Scandalized Exit
- **Downfall:** Disgraced, Destroyed
- **Secret:** Kingmaker, Redemption, Untouchable, Truth & Reconciliation
