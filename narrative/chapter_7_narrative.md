# Chapter 7: Resolution

**Location:** Senator's Office, Capitol Hill  
**Time:** 9:00 AM, Day 7  
**Mood:** Decisive, final

The week that began with routine briefings ends with your career on the line. Every relationship you've built leads to this moment.

Maria, Laura, Emma, Jessica, Olivia, Victoria, Isabella, Natalie, and Ashley—each has played a role in your journey.

## Your Final Choice

1. **Fight and stay in the race** (Determined ending)
   - Requires: High reputation, moderate risk
   
2. **Step back temporarily** (Cautious ending)
   - Requires: Medium reputation, low-medium risk
   
3. **Pivot to a different role** (Strategic ending)
   - Requires: Low reputation, low risk
   
4. **Retire from politics** (Final ending)
   - Triggered by: Very low reputation, high risk

## Four Possible Endings

### Victory
High reputation (70+), low risk (30-). You emerge stronger. The scandal becomes a footnote. Your legacy is secured.

### Damaged but Surviving
Medium reputation (50-69), medium risk (31-50). You survive but are weakened. Future ambitions require rebuilding.

### Strategic Retreat
Low reputation (<50), low risk (<40). You step aside gracefully. Perhaps a comeback another day.

### Downfall
Very low reputation (<30), high risk (50+). Your career ends in disgrace. The scandal defines you.
