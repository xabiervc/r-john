# R-John: Public Servant — Day 1 Playable Vertical Slice

## What this demo is

A short **graphic-adventure** slice, not a dialogue-only prototype. You control R. John in his office, inspect and collect objects, talk to Emma, Laura and Maria, prepare for a debate, confront a rival candidate and receive a consequence summary.

## Run it

1. Checkout `feat/godot-vertical-slice`.
2. Open `project.godot` with **Godot 4.7.2**.
3. Run with F6/F5.
4. Move with arrow keys or WASD; press **E** near highlighted people and objects.

## Day 1 flow

1. Explore the office as R. John.
2. Collect the debate briefing from the desk.
3. Optionally inspect the archive for the rival's record and the phone for a donor card.
4. Have short contextual conversations with Emma, Laura and Maria.
5. Speak with Maria after collecting the briefing.
6. Leave through the door to enter the debate.
7. Use preparation, evidence and relationships to unlock debate replies.
8. Reach the Day 1 summary.

## Systems demonstrated

- Player movement and spatial interactions.
- R. John and three staff members visible in the office.
- Objects: briefing, archive, phone, exit door.
- Inventory and persistent Day 1 state.
- Brief, branchable conversations with boundary and risk consequences.
- Debate scene with moderator, rival candidate and conditional responses.
- Save/load and restart.

## Visual scope

All characters and office props are intentionally geometric placeholders. Final art, portraits, animation, music, SFX, screen-reader behavior and full accessibility settings remain production work.
